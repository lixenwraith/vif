Controls

Tap
Move the cursor there and fire the main weapon; hold to repeat
Drag
Drag the cursor with your finger
Double tap
Fire the special weapon as well
Hold the status bar
Open the configuration menu: audio, bots, multiplayer and more
Two fingers, tap twice
Show or hide the keyboard, to type commands such as :help
Pinch
Zoom in or out; the zoom is kept for next time
The strip at the top
Keyboard, Tab, Esc while typing, sound and full screen

With a keyboard, F1 or :help lists every key and command, and :q quits.

Move the mouse
The cursor follows it; :free turns that off or on
Click
Move the cursor there and fire the main weapon; hold to repeat
Right click
Fire the special weapon where the cursor already is; hold to repeat
Hold the status bar
Open the configuration menu: audio, bots, multiplayer and more; Ctrl-G or F10 too
Ctrl and the wheel
Zoom in or out, as a trackpad's pinch does; the zoom is kept for next time
h j k l, the arrows
Move one cell left, down, up, right
Enter, Space
Fire the main weapon, the special one
Ctrl-S
Cycle audio: music and effects, music only, effects only, silent

F1 or :help lists every key and command, and :q quits.
